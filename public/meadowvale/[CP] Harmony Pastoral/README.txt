Harmony Pastoral

This Content Patcher pack is deliberately asset-free until source sprite files are supplied.
Add recolored PNGs under assets/spring, assets/summer, assets/fall, or assets/winter, then add matching Content Patcher edits to content.json.

Requirements:
- Stardew Valley
- SMAPI 4.5.2 or newer
- Content Patcher 2.x or newer

The official SMAPI installer is in ../smapi-installer/SMAPI 4.5.2 installer.
SMAPI cannot be installed automatically here because no Stardew Valley game directory was found.
